SnippetPhone

Welcome to SnippetPhone

SnippetPhone is a browser-to-browser voice call. It records just the snippets with voice and ensures they get sent. This improves calls in poor network conditions.

To make the demo work, the app needs microphone access so it can listen for your voice and send only the moments you speak. This is used only for your conversation and is not stored or transmitted elsewhere.

If you deny microphone access, the app will break and you won't be able to make voice calls.
